What fractional ownership means

Investors may own shares or another defined interest in an entity that holds a property. The exact structure determines voting rights, income distributions, fees, transfer limits and how a sale is handled. Those rights must be set out in the offer documents; a percentage shown on a screen is not enough.

Due diligence checklist

Verify the asset

Title, valuation, condition, location, insurance and any debt secured against the property.

Understand the numbers

Rental assumptions, vacancy, maintenance, taxes, management fees and sensitivity to lower income.

Read the rights

Entity documents, voting, distributions, reporting, transfers, complaints and conflict handling.

Plan for risk and exit

Property values and income can fall. Shares may be hard to sell, and invested capital can be lost.
